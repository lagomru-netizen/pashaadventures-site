How Does a Steam Train Work?

Pasha and Anya travel to Grandma's on a real steam train! On the way they find out why the engine goes "choo-choo", what's inside it, who drives the train, what signals and water towers are for — and who invented the steam engine.

▶  Watch on YouTube

Audio: English · Русский · Español · Português · हिन्दी

What your child will learn

  • Steam comes from boiling water — just like a kettle
  • The firebox, the boiler and the pistons turn the wheels
  • The driver and the fireman work together in the cab
  • Signals and water towers keep the train safe on its way
  • The steam engine was invented over 200 years ago

Talk about it

Questions to ask after watching — there are no wrong answers.

  • “What makes the steam in a steam engine?”
  • “Why does the fireman add coal to the fire?”
  • “Where else do you see steam at home?”
  • “If you drove a train, where would you go?”
